Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use this site (pages visited, approximate location, device type). This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you. Where your data lives

Email and scheduling run on Zoho (EU data centres). Analytics data is processed by Google. This site is served via Cloudflare. Each provider processes data under its own privacy terms as our processor.

Correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any engagement that follows, then archived or deleted. Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics' standard retention settings.

Cookies

The only non-essential cookies on this site are Google Analytics cookies, controlled by the consent banner. You can change your choice by clearing this site's cookies in your browser.
