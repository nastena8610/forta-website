Your questions, answered

Business process automation in the UAE: FAQs.

Can business processes in my company be automated?

Processes with repetitive tasks, clear rules and regular movement of information are useful starting points. CRM updates, task creation, document requests and reminders are examples. Forta reviews the workflow first to identify what can be automated and what should remain with people.

Where should I start with business process optimization?

Start with one recurring process that creates delays, repeated data entry or unnecessary follow-ups. In a free 30-minute process review, Forta maps how it works today and identifies practical improvement opportunities.

Do I need AI for every automation?

No. A clearer workflow or rules-based automation may be enough. Forta considers AI where it supports a specific task and a clear business case, while keeping human judgement where it creates value.

Which businesses does Forta UAE help?

Forta for UAE focuses on small and medium-sized businesses in the United Arab Emirates. Its process work covers operations, sales, recruitment, client delivery and back-office workflows.

What happens after the free process review?

You receive a practical first view of what to keep, systemize and automate. If there is a clear business case, Forta can help design and implement the solution. You can stop after the review; the first call is free and carries no commitment.

How is the calculator estimate calculated?

Annual manual-work cost equals the number of people multiplied by hours per person per week, 52 weeks and hourly cost in AED. Potential time-cost released applies your selected reduction percentage. This is a directional estimate of time-cost, not a guarantee of cash savings; implementation costs are not included.
