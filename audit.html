What a finding looks like.

Candidate submission → client feedback

What you told me
Feedback arrives by email or phone. Recruiters chase it and record it in different places.

What I would examine
No single owner or agreed time for feedback; the ATS record is often behind the conversation.

First change to test
Assign one owner for each submission and log feedback in one place within an agreed time window. Measure how many submissions need a second chase.
